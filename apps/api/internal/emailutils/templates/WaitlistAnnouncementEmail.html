Hey Hacker,

We know our last email wasn't the news you were hoping for, but as promised, the SwampHacks XII waitlist is now open!

How to join the waitlist

  1. Log in to the Hacker Portal
  2. Select the option to join the waitlist
  3. That's it! You'll be added to the waitlist automatically.
Join the Waitlist

The deadline to join is October 15 at 11:59 PM ET.

How it works

  • First come, first served: Your place on the waitlist is based on when you opt in, so the sooner you join, the better your chances.
  • We'll notify you: If a spot opens up, we'll email you an invitation. You'll have 48 hours to accept it, or the spot will go to the next hacker in line. Keep an eye on your inbox (and your spam folder)!
  • Day-of waitlist: On the day of the event, we'll open an in-person waitlist, and hackers on the in-person waitlist will be prioritized. If you're on the online waitlist and still hoping to attend, we recommend joining the in-person waitlist on event day.
  • Change of plans? You can remove yourself from the waitlist at any time through the Hacker Portal.

Joining the waitlist doesn't guarantee a spot, but hackers come off the waitlist every year, so don't count yourself out!

Whether or not you come off the waitlist, you can still be part of SwampHacks XII as a volunteer or mentor:

Thanks again for your interest in SwampHacks. We hope to see you there!
